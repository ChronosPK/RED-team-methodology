# T10 — Letter of Authorisation

> **Authority record.** This letter records target-specific consent and the operational
> authority represented by its signatories. It is necessary but may not be sufficient:
> applicable law, regulation, contract, third-party rights and provider conditions remain
> in force. The legal adviser shall confirm the required authorisation chain for the actual
> jurisdiction, entities, systems and activities. This letter shall be:
>
> - Signed by the person with **actual system authority** for every system or facility in
>   scope, with operational risk countersigned where that authority is held separately.
> - Short enough to be read and understood in under a minute by a police officer, a
>   security guard, or a systems administrator.
> - Comprehensible without reference to the Rules of Engagement. It stands alone.
> - Carried by every operator, physically and digitally, for the whole engagement.
>
> **Verify each signatory's authority.** A person without the relevant authority cannot
> confer valid system consent or risk acceptance. If systems belong to another legal
> entity, division, agency or third party, obtain that authority directly and record it in
> the ROE. Do not infer authority from title alone.

# Immediate-Verification Card

> Separate from the Letter of Authorisation. Carried by each operator, particularly for
> physical or on-site work.
>
> **Contains nothing but the following.** No scope, no techniques, no target list, no ROE
> extract. Its only purpose is de-escalation and rapid verification by someone who has just
> detained the operator. Any additional detail is intelligence handed to whoever is holding
> it.

| Verification detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Status | **AUTHORISED SECURITY TESTING IN PROGRESS** |
| Bearer | The bearer is conducting authorised security testing on behalf of [ORGANISATION]. |
| Engagement reference | [RT-YYYY-NNN] |
| Approving Authority | [APPROVING AUTHORITY NAME] |
| Telephone, 24 hours | [TELEPHONE] |
| Trusted Agent | [TRUSTED AGENT NAME] |
| Telephone, 24 hours | [TELEPHONE] |
| Immediate action | Call to verify before taking further action. |

## Operator instructions if challenged

1. **Stop immediately.** Do not resist, evade, or leave.
2. State clearly: *"I am conducting authorised security testing for [ORGANISATION]."*
3. Produce the verification card. Ask that the number be called.
4. Comply with all instructions given by security personnel or law enforcement.
5. Do not disclose scope, techniques, targets, or the ROE.
6. Contact the Red Team Lead and Trusted Agent as soon as permitted.
7. Record what happened as soon as possible afterwards.

**Where physical entry is authorised**, the Trusted Agent shall confirm before execution
that site security leadership and, where appropriate, local law enforcement liaison have
been briefed that authorised activity is occurring in the period — without disclosing
timing or method.
