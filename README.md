# Red Team Methodology

The governing publication is [RED-TEAM-METHODOLOGY.md](RED-TEAM-METHODOLOGY.md). The
remaining documents are subordinate to it.

---

## Structure

| | Function | Amendment | Signed |
|---|---|---|---|
| [RED-TEAM-METHODOLOGY.md](RED-TEAM-METHODOLOGY.md) | The governing publication. Eleven chapters and a lexicon: definitions, aim, authority, appointments, conduct of an engagement, standing instructions, measures, resources, governance. | Amendment to a requirement expressed as "shall" requires re-signature | Yes |
| Annexes A to K | Operational and technical detail | Issued by the Head of Red Team | No |
| Documents T01 to T12 | Completed for each engagement and retained as part of the engagement record | Issued by the Head of Red Team | No |

The separation is deliberate. Content subject to change, including tooling, techniques,
infrastructure and technique tables, is held in the annexes so that the governing
publication does not require re-signature when it changes.

Organisational particulars appear only in the pro formas, where they are completed for each
engagement.

## Introduction of the capability

| Week | Activity |
|---|---|
| 1 | Identify the applicable legal instruments under Annex G, paragraph G.2, through the legal adviser |
| 1 to 3 | Obtain approval of the publication. Appoint the Approving Authority and Trusted Agent. |
| 2 to 4 | Construct the laboratory, per Annex J, paragraph J1.2 |
| 4 | Adopt pro formas T02 and T10 |
| 5 | Conduct one purple team exercise, per Annex H, part H4 |
| 6 to 10 | Conduct one scoped assumed-breach engagement |
| 11 to 14 | Report, debrief, track remediation, and retest |

The two failures that most commonly end a programme are commencing with a full-scope covert
engagement, and never conducting the retest cycle.
